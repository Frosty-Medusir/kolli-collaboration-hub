# Kolli v1.0 — Release notes
12 November 2026

- Discover movies and TV shows through live TMDB search.
- Rate titles privately and receive personalized recommendations.
- Match with someone watching the same title and get a private Kolli room automatically.
- Chat with spam and harassment filtering; rate or report your partner.
- Adjust recommendation weights and disliked-genre filtering from the admin page.
- Export your data or delete your account.

**Known limitations:** Kosmi video rooms still require manual links. Chat filtering is not foolproof. Permanent admin access and the operator’s privacy contact must be set before public launch.

These notes describe v1.0 functionality; they do not confirm a public deployment.
