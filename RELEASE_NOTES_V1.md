# Kolli v1.2 — Release notes
12 November 2026 · Kodovoz Labs

## New
- **AI chat checks:** every message is checked before it's sent, catching disguised abuse and spam.
- **Privacy:** the admin email is no longer in the public code; no private keys are exposed.
- **Phone layout:** shorter top bar, sideways-scrolling category buttons, smaller posters.
- **Viewing styles:** Chatty, Silent Watcher, Mic On or Text Only; clashing styles aren't paired.
- **Save for later:** up to 5 titles for 7 days, with an in-app alert when a match turns up.
- **Badges:** Great Chat, Good Vibes and Movie Buff after watching.
- **Icebreakers:** movie trivia opens every new chat.
- **Live events:** sports, esports and finales posted by admins.
- **Teleparty & Kolli rooms:** watch in a private room or share a Teleparty link.
- **Safety:** 24-hour guest IP log and a browser check that limits returning banned users.
- **Admin:** reports, ban/unban, ranking settings, Sign in with Lovable.
- **Install:** add Kolli to your phone's home screen.

## Known limits
- Alerts are in-app only; email needs your own domain.
- Kosmi and Teleparty links are created by hand.
- No chat filter is perfect; the report button remains the backup.
