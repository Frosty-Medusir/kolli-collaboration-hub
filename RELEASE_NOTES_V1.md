**Release Notes V.1
**Viewing styles: pick Chatty, Silent Watcher, Mic On or Text Only; clashing styles aren't paired.
Save for later: save up to 5 titles for 7 days and get an in-app alert when a match turns up.
Badges: give partners positive badges (Great Chat, Good Vibes, Movie Buff) after watching.
Icebreakers: a piece of movie trivia opens every new chat.
Live events: sports, esports and reality finales, posted by admins.
Teleparty: share a Teleparty link for Netflix or Prime nights.
Safety: 24-hour guest IP log and browser check that quietly limits returning banned users.
Admin: moderation dashboard with reports and ban/unban; Sign in with Lovable.
Phone: animated loading screen, app icon, install to home screen, and a tidier phone layout.

Known limits
Alerts are in-app only; email needs your own domain.
Kosmi and Teleparty links are created by hand.
The browser check can be bypassed; AI chat checks catch disguised abuse but no filter is perfect.
